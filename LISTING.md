## Name

Ask AI by OneTag

## Byline

Answer visitor questions from your own pages.

## Description

Add an Ask AI box to every page of your site. Visitors type a question and get a short answer written from your published pages, with links to the pages it used.

Paste your free site key, pick a label, color and position, and click "Add to site". The plugin adds one script tag to your site's custom code. Publish, and the box is live. Click "Remove" to take it out.

- Answers come from your own content, with links back to it.
- Works on every page, no components to place.
- Free plan: 1,000,000 tokens a month with a hard cap. No bill can appear.
- The key is public by design, like an analytics ID.

Get a key at https://onetagai.com/?ref=framer#get-key
